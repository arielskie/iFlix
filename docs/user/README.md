## 🎬 Viewer Feature Highlights

- **Seamless Netflix Dark Mode**: Engineered for immersive viewing in low-light environments with high-contrast text and crisp typography.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Hover previews with autoplaying muted trailers, animated loading skeletons, and fluid slide transitions.
- **Cross-Device Continuity**: Switch from watching a series on your mobile phone to your Smart TV or desktop browser without losing your timestamp.
